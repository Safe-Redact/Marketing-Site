## Format boundary

- Document/image inputs promoted here: PDF; PNG, JPG/JPEG, TIFF, BMP; HTML, Markdown, RTF, TXT, DOCX; additional office/OpenDocument formats when LibreOffice is available. PST/OST is not promoted pending representative acceptance.
- Audio inputs implemented in code: WAV, MP3, M4A, AAC, FLAC, OGG/OGA, OPUS, WMA, AIFF/AIF, ALAC, AMR. Output support is narrower and FFmpeg-dependent.
- Video input containers implemented in code: MP4/M4V, MOV, AVI, MKV, WebM.

## Blocked claims

The registry and tests block named compliance/certification, customer proof, numeric accuracy or speed, universal offline processing, cloud collaboration/RBAC/integrations, automatic transcript analysis as a complete shipped workflow, perfect synchronization, generic/full-motion tracking, live redaction, and guaranteed/irreversible/error-free output claims.

## Validation and release rule

`scripts/validate-content.ts` validates the typed feature registry during prebuild. Every published page must have allowlisted claim IDs, and no published page may reference a blocked claim. Conditional wording must retain its configuration, source-quality, tooling, or reviewer-responsibility qualifier. Deployment remains prohibited until the owner accepts this branch and any listed representative-file gate needed for the intended deployment.

## Multipage and Industries usage (2026-10-26)

No new capability rows were added. Industry guides (`/industries/*`), `/how-it-works`, and `/why-openredakt` reuse the IDs above; they do not introduce new product claims.

- **Industry workflows** list claim IDs per format in `src/content/industries.ts`. The page renders each claim's `publicWording` and `condition` verbatim from `capabilityClaims`, so a wording change here changes every industry page. Validation rejects a blocked ID, or an ID from another format (for example a `VID-` claim on a document workflow).
- **How It Works** stages cite DOC/AUD/VID IDs in `src/content/pages.ts`. Stage copy restates conditions (OCR, LibreOffice, FFmpeg, configured engines) in plain language.
- **Blocked wording** for industry and supporting copy is enforced by `blockedIndustryCopy`. Regulation names are excluded entirely, along with compliance, certification, guarantee, irreversibility, percentage, speed, tracking, adoption, and log-in or free-trial language.
- **Drafts.** `real-estate` and `ai-data-teams` are held as drafts (reasons are recorded in the registry). They render 404 and are absent from navigation and the sitemap.
- **PST/OST.** The Education guide states that mailbox files are not promoted as an input, consistent with the format boundary above.

Product acceptance with sector-representative files remains a pre-deployment gate wherever a cited claim is Conditional.
